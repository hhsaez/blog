- [Hellboy: Mignola's Genius for Simplification](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NaiGf8xm4Hs)
  - A great study on Mike Mignola's art and how he simplifies complex scenes. I specially like how he uses pure black silhouettes to create a sort of "depth of field" effect and draw the attention to where it is needed.
- Termine de leer los seis volumenes del manga de Akira. Y justo a tiempo para ir a ver el reestreno en el cine.
- [10 Ways to Improve Game Feel - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qCj9CZoAvFY)
